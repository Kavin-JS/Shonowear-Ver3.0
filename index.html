The Problem

Fashion discovery
is broken.

Endless product grids, zero curation. You scroll for hours and still can't find your aesthetic. Generic storefronts don't understand culture — they just list SKUs.

No cultural context behind products
Cluttered feeds with no visual hierarchy
Zero style discovery — just catalogs
Old way — just a catalog
The Solution

Style discovery,
not just shopping.

Shonowear is a fashion discovery platform built for culture — not commerce. Every piece is curated with context, every collection tells a story.

Curated Discovery

Hand-picked collections organized by anime universe, style aesthetic, and cultural moment.

Image-First Browsing

Visual-first layouts inspired by editorial fashion — browse like a lookbook, not a spreadsheet.

Smart Filtering

Filter by universe, style, price, and type. Find exactly what fits your aesthetic in seconds.

Drop Culture

Limited edition releases, new arrivals, and exclusive drops — all surfaced before they sell out.

Platform Features

Built different.
By design.

Dynamic Product Grid

Live-filtered, search-driven, category-aware grid that rerenders instantly without a page load.

Multi-Filter System

Filter by type, price range, anime universe, and sort order — simultaneously.

Persistent Cart

localStorage-powered cart that survives page refreshes, with quantity management and live totals.

Auth System

Session-based login with protected user state across pages.

Live Search

Instant search with debounce — finds products as you type.
